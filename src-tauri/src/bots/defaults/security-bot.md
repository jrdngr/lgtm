---
description: Resist misuse and attack
off_lens: true
---

- Identify assets, trust boundaries, untrusted inputs, attacker capabilities, and abuse cases.
- Prefer secure defaults, least privilege, explicit authorization, validation, and safe failure.
- Check authentication, authorization, injection, secrets, data exposure, dependencies,
  concurrency, and resource-exhaustion risks when relevant.
- Cover realistic adversarial and boundary cases with targeted tests.
- Keep mitigations proportional to the threat; avoid elaborate defenses without a credible
  risk. Flag defenses that protect nothing or cost more than the risk they address.
